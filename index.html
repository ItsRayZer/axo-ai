Try it live

Click the chat button at the bottom-right of this page. Ask it about prices, hours, or services — it answers from the demo FAQ. Then open ⚙ Settings inside the chat and paste your own API key to switch it to real AI.

Why Axo Chat?

🔑 Works without a key

Out of the box, Axo answers from your FAQ list — perfect for small business sites that need a helper today, not after an API signup.

🧠 Real AI when you want it

Paste an OpenAI-compatible or Gemini key in the widget settings and Axo switches to live AI answers instantly.

🎨 Fully yours

Bot name, avatar, colours, theme, welcome message, suggested questions, FAQ answers — everything is one config file.

↺ Reset anytime

Visitors can reset the conversation or wipe everything, and history stays only in their own browser.

📱 Mobile-first

Clean, fast, responsive panel that looks native on phones and desktops.

🔒 Private by design

Keys are stored in the visitor's browser only — never on an Axo server (there isn't one).

Add it to your website in 30 seconds

Copy these two files into your site, then paste this before </body>:

<script src="axo.config.js"></script>
<script src="dist/axo-chat.js"></script>

That's it. Edit axo.config.js to make it yours: your business name, your FAQ answers, your colours.

Customise everything

Every option lives in axo.config.js with comments: bot name, avatar, theme (light / dark / auto), accent colour, panel position, welcome message, suggestion chips, FAQ brain, AI provider, model, system prompt, and behaviour flags like history persistence and the reset button.

Prefer data attributes? This works too:

<script src="dist/axo-chat.js"
  data-bot-name="ShopBot"
  data-accent-color="#e91e63"
  data-theme="dark"></script>
